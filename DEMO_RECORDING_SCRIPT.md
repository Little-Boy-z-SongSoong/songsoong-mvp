# SongSoong — live demo recording script

**Target length:** 4:15–4:45. Record the browser at [songsoong-mvp.vercel.app](https://songsoong-mvp.vercel.app/) in English, with system audio enabled. The quoted text is the spoken narration. The actions are screen directions, not lines to read. Let both music clips play without speaking over them.

## 0:00–0:30 — The idea

**Screen:** Show the opening section, the OneAquaHealth credit, and the five city cards.

> “Every river has a melody. SongSoong is our interactive story about urban waterways, built for OneAquaHealth's awareness and storytelling track. Ecological observations are valuable, but a table of ratings can feel distant. We let visitors hear a place first, then trace what they heard back to the published observation. The sound is an artistic interpretation, not a water-safety test.”

## 0:30–1:35 — Hear the difference

**Screen:** Click **Hear the difference**. Show Coimbra A and B. Play **Listen to A** and **Listen to B** for about 12 seconds each. Keep the cursor away from the cards while the music plays.

> “Here are two real macroinvertebrate observations from Coimbra, collected on the same day. Both compositions use Coimbra's musical palette, but their ecological rating and recorded richness change what we hear. Listen to A and B. Which site shows more ecological pressure?”

**Pause narration while A and B play.**

## 1:35–2:05 — Reveal the evidence

**Screen:** Choose **B** and show the revealed records. Point to the site codes, ratings, richness and date.

> “B is Corujeira, site C20: Poor, with recorded richness of seventeen. A is Conraria, site C17: Good, with richness of thirty-nine. Both were observed on June sixth, twenty twenty-three. The poorer rating makes B's harmony more tense and its pulse heavier. Lower richness means fewer small water-drop accents. These ratings describe the invertebrate group; they do not tell us whether the water is safe to drink.”

## 2:05–2:55 — Explore a real sampling site

**Screen:** Click **Explore C20 record**. Show the site selector, available signals, published value and date. Play the whole waterway mix, then isolate one available biological signal. Scroll to the source records.

> “The challenge leads into a full portrait of a sampling site. Each available signal can be heard on its own, and the interface keeps its published rating or value and observation date visible. Visitors can also open the location on a map and inspect the source rows. The point is to make the music explainable: every data-driven change should lead back to a record, while the city theme remains a clearly composed choice.”

## 2:55–3:35 — Five distinct city voices

**Screen:** Switch to another city, such as Oslo or Toulouse, and play a short excerpt. Return to Coimbra if helpful.

> “SongSoong covers Oslo, Benevento, Ghent, Toulouse, and Coimbra. Each city has its own instrument, scale, chords, motif, and rhythm. A sampling site adds a repeatable variation, and its observations shape the musical condition. We wanted listeners to recognize a place and hear ecological differences, rather than hear the same tune with a few notes rearranged.”

## 3:35–4:15 — Mapping, limits, and participation

**Screen:** Show the three mapping cards, move the musical-tension slider once, then show the source-data section and OneAquaHealth Citizen Science link. End on the SongSoong title and URL.

> “The mapping stays visible: biological ratings affect harmony and tension; recorded richness changes accent density; nitrate adds a subtle colour based only on its relative position in this dataset. The API does not provide a nitrate unit or safety threshold here, so we do not label a value safe or unsafe. The remix slider is a hypothetical musical experiment and never changes the published observation. SongSoong reads OneAquaHealth's ENORA data, works from a saved snapshot when the API is unavailable, and creates sound in the browser. Visitors who want to take part can continue through the official OneAquaHealth Citizen Science App. SongSoong was built by Little Boy'z with sponsorship from Lunawave Technologies, LunaTech. Listen, compare, understand—and check the source.”
